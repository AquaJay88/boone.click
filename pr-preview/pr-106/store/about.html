About JB Craftsman

Crafting quality with intention.

Our Philosophy: Beyond "Good Enough"

Each of our products started as a frustration. Whether it’s a cluttered game box or a struggling event setup, I’ve always been driven by one question: How can I make this better? I don't believe in "one size fits all" solutions that only get the job done halfway. I believe in maximizing the potential of every object we use daily.

The Origin Story: The Search for a Better Sign

My journey into intentional design began with a simple problem at work. We hosted frequent vegetarian receptions and were relying on messy chalkboard signs. I needed a way to clearly communicate if a dish was also vegan, gluten-Free, or both. All while setting up in the final, frantic minutes before an event.

The options I found online were either overpriced or didn't fit our specific needs. So, I decided to build something that worked for us. I designed our Vegetarian Table Signs to be adaptable, compact for travel, and instantly readable. That project ignited a passion for not settling with something that was 'good enough', but creating something better.

The Process: Where Intentionality Meets Precision

While the heavy lifting and finishing touches happen in my workshop, the "soul" of the design happens at my dining room table. While listening to a good playlist and petting my adorable dog, I spend hours sketching and iterating on ideas.

I utilize 3D printing as my primary tool for one specific reason: it allows me to tailor a design to fit an exact need with a high level of intentionality. From that first layer to the final hand-finished product, the goal is always the same. To crafting something that makes day-to-day life run a little smoother.

Built for the Efficient Life

JB Craftsman is for the organizers, the optimizers, and anyone who appreciates a product that does exactly what it says it will do. I’m glad you’re here, and I hope these designs help you find a little more efficiency in your own world.
